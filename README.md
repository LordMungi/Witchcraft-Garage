# Witchcraft Garage
## Summary:

 Your trusty Crow delivers you a letter with a request for a potion, read it carefully and search in your storage for the best ingredients to satisfy your customers! 

## Tutorial:

 There are 10 feelings that customers will seek; Calm, Anxiety, Love, Heartbreak, Adulthood, Nostalgia, Hapiness, Sadness, Energy and Tiredness. As a witch with a garage potion shop you must find the perfect items that represent the emotion the customer is looking for and mix them together to make a potion. When the delivery is complete, you will recieve a review based on your performance. 

## Gameplay:

First, you click on the crow and it will give you the request, then you mix the items in the pot, and when you are finished click te check button and you are all set! In case you make a mistake just click the rewind button and it will reset your cauldron.
